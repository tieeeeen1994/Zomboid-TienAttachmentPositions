# Tien's Attachment Positions

Move, turn and resize what hangs on your character, and every other player sees it on you.

- **Any slot.** Knives on the belt, pistols in holsters, rifles and pans on the back, and every slot that bag and
  attachment mods add (Plysken Attachments Reborn included). Right-click an attached item, in the inventory or on the
  hotbar, and pick **Adjust position**.
- **Move, turn, resize.** Sliders for three directions of movement, three of rotation, and size. The item moves as
  you drag.
- **Yours alone.** Your settings only change your own character. Another player's settings only change theirs, so
  everyone can set up their own look.
- **Seen by everyone.** In multiplayer the server keeps every player's settings and sends them to everyone, so other
  players see your items where you put them. They stay after relogging, a server restart and a new character.
- **Per slot.** A setting belongs to the hotbar slot, so every item you hang there follows it, a different weapon you
  put there later and the with-bag spots on the back included. Moving a slider shows the change on your screen
  only; **Save** stores it for the whole slot and every player sees it. **Reset** shows where the game hangs things and
  **Restore** goes back to your last save; neither saves by itself. Closing with unsaved changes asks whether to save
  them. **Reset all slots** (top right) asks first, then puts every slot back at once.

## Notes

- **Plysken Attachments Reborn:** that mod has its own *Adjust attachment position* option. While this mod is on, that
  option is hidden and *Adjust position* takes its place. Positions you set with Plysken's option are not used, so set
  them again with this one. Plysken's positions are saved on your own computer, so only you see them, and they move
  that spot on every character you see, not only on yours.
- Two vanilla spots, the shoulder and ankle holsters, cannot be moved: the game hides them under some clothing by their
  name, and moving them would stop that.
- The window fits any font size.

Build 42 only. Must be installed on the server and on every client. Safe to add to an existing save; removing it puts
every item back on its usual spot.
