description=[list]
description=[*][b]Any slot:[/b] knives on the belt, pistols in holsters, rifles and pans on the back, and every slot that bag and attachment mods add (Plysken Attachments Reborn included). Right-click an attached item, in the inventory or on the hotbar, and pick [i]Adjust position[/i].
description=[*][b]Move, turn, resize:[/b] sliders for three directions of movement, three of rotation, and size. The item moves as you drag.
description=[*][b]Yours alone:[/b] your settings only change your own character. Another player's settings only change theirs, so everyone can set up their own look.
description=[*][b]Seen by everyone:[/b] in multiplayer the server keeps every player's settings and sends them to everyone, so other players see your items where you put them. They stay after relogging, a server restart and a new character.
description=[*][b]Per slot:[/b] a setting belongs to the hotbar slot, so every item you hang there follows it, a different weapon you put there later and the with-bag spots on the back included. Sliders show the change on your screen only; [i]Save[/i] stores it for the whole slot and every player sees it. Closing without saving puts it back. Reset one slot or all of them at any time (it asks first).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Plysken Attachments Reborn:[/b] that mod has its own [i]Adjust attachment position[/i] option. While this mod is on, that option is hidden and [i]Adjust position[/i] takes its place. Positions you set with Plysken's option are not used, so set them again with this one. Plysken's positions are saved on your own computer, so only you see them, and they move that spot on every character you see, not only on yours.
description=[*]Two vanilla spots, the shoulder and ankle holsters, cannot be moved: the game hides them under some clothing by their name, and moving them would stop that.
description=[*]The window fits any font size.
description=[/list]
